## Context

Compared exec and eng modes end-to-end. The exec view has a tight 4-tab persuasion arc (Summary → Scale → Security → Roadmap) with consistent structure: sidebar subsections for in-page navigation, emerald "Bottom Line" closers on every tab, and contiguous §1–§4 numbering. The eng view has 8 disconnected reference pages with mismatched section numbers, no sidebar subsections, no consistent closing pattern, and no narrative connective tissue.

### Key findings:

1. **Section numbers are broken.** Badge numbers on views (3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10) don't match the `ROADMAP_ENG` chip numbers (§1–§8) or the sidebar menu order. Four of eight views have mismatches.

2. **No sidebar subsections.** Exec menu items have `sections` arrays for in-page anchor scrolling. Eng menu items have zero — long pages like Enforcement (452 lines, 3 major sections) have no way to jump within the page.

3. **No "Bottom Line" closers.** Exec views all end with a styled emerald "The Bottom Line" block. Eng views end inconsistently — some have CalloutBoxes, some have nothing.

4. **No narrative connectors.** Each eng page acts as a standalone document. No "as established in §2" framing, no forward references from overview to deeper pages.

5. **Overview front-loads everything.** The eng overview renders 8 content blocks including the full `EntitlementsDiagram`, making deeper pages feel redundant rather than essential.

6. **Menu labels don't match view titles.** Menu says "Hardening" but badge says "Section 3" and title says "System-Based Enforcement". Menu says "Developer Experience" but badge says "Section 2 · Developer Experience".

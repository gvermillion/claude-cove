# Triage Log

Manual tracking of draft quality across sessions. Update after each send/skip decision.

## How to Use

After each session, record outcomes in the table below.
- **approved** — sent as-is or with only minor wording tweaks
- **edited** — substantially rewritten before sending
- **rejected** — discarded; wrote manually
- **skipped** — decided not to reply at all
